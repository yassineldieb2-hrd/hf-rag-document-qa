Return Policy

Customers can return unused items within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Used items can only be returned within 14 days and receive store credit instead of a refund.

Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 days and costs 15 zloty extra. Orders over 200 zloty get free standard shipping.

Warranty

All electronics come with a 2-year warranty. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but not damage caused by water or drops.

Support

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. You can contact us at support@example.com.
